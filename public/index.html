Taxi peak hours are taken as 8–10am and 4–8pm on weekdays and 4pm–midnight on weekends. Average road speed drops to about 26 km/h in weekday peaks, which adds taxi waiting time and raises fuel use. Distance, traffic and the Salik gates passed come from the fastest Google Maps route at your departure time; hours other than the one you picked use the speed model. Al Maktoum Bridge is free on Sundays and 10pm–6am. If Google Maps is unavailable, distance falls back to straight-line × 1.25.
